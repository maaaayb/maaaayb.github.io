About Stillpoint

A quiet studio built for real rest.

Stillpoint began with a simple idea: that good bodywork shouldn't feel like a factory line. Here, you get the full hour, an unhurried therapist, and a space designed to help your shoulders drop the moment you walk in.

Every session is personalized. Tell us where you hold stress and what you want to feel afterward, and we build the treatment around that.

  • — Warm, private treatment room with adjustable heat and music
  • — Organic, unscented oils available for sensitive skin
  • — Easy online booking and honest, no-upsell pricing
